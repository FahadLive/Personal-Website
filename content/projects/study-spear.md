**Study Spear** is an adaptive-learning personal web app from **Team Experia**, focused on productivity & education.

## 💡 The idea

- **AI-powered energy-aware task scheduling** — learns the user's biological and behavioral peak hours through an onboarding questionnaire and adapts over time
- **Smart content prioritization with adaptive flashcards** — highlights the most relevant and exam-focused points, especially those less familiar to the learner
