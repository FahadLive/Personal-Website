Saylo is a platform that helps students sell items they no longer find useful but that might still be valuable to others. By streamlining the buying and selling process, it fosters a sustainable and efficient exchange of resources within the campus.

## 🎯 Goals

- Promote reuse of stuff
- Help students save money

## 📋 Plan

- An easy-to-use platform with intuitive tagging (e.g. `#year1`, `#cse`, `#mech`)
- User registration with year, department, and more
- Categorization for products (stationery, textbooks, records, …)
- A reputation system for sellers — price tracking, buyer/seller authentication
- An admin dashboard for feedback review and platform monitoring

Built with **React** (`reactjs`), deployed as a **webapp**. Design is done, frontend/backend development on the roadmap.
