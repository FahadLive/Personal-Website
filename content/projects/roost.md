**Roost** is a mobile-first digital solution that streamlines hostel-related operations such as student registration, attendance, and food scheduling. It offers a centralized platform for both students and administrators to interact seamlessly and manage hostel life efficiently. Made with **Flutter**. This is a fork of [Muflih-uk/Roost](https://github.com/Muflih-uk/Roost).

## 👨‍🎓 Student view

- Dashboard with attendance and meal tracking
- Attendance toggle (In/Out) to mark hostel presence, with history
- Pre-schedule food availability and overview of meal preferences

## 🛠️ Admin dashboard (in plans)

- Student management (registration, room moves, removal)
- Attendance overview for individual students

## 🧰 Tech stack

- Frontend: Flutter (mobile)
- Backend: Express.js (Node.js)
- Database: MongoDB Atlas
- Authentication: username & passwords (OTP-based login planned)
