The student-run website of the **Computer Science and Engineering Department** at **GEC Sreekrishnapuram** — a one-stop destination for academic programs, events, and student resources. Forked from [CSEGECPKD/cse_dept_site](https://github.com/CSEGECPKD/cse_dept_site).

## ✨ Key features

- Department overview — mission, vision, and objectives
- Academic programs with syllabus, curriculum, and admission details
- Research and projects highlights
- Events & news updates
- Resources for students (course materials, schedules, timetables)
- Achievements, recognitions, and a vibrant gallery

Built with **Next.js** and styled with **Tailwind CSS**, deployed at [cse-site-one.vercel.app](https://cse-site-one.vercel.app).
