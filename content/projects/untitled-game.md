A strategy/puzzle game inspired by **Bad Piggies**, made with **Godot**.

## 📖 Plot (might change)

You play as a hornbill who needs to feed his family, enclosed in the trunk of a tree. One day, while foraging for food, you get hurt and can't really fly anymore. Now you have to build contraptions with the items at hand to gather food for your family.
